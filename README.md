Vrinda Store Sales Analysis

📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes Vrinda Store’s 2022 product sales data using Microsoft Excel. The analysis was conducted to understand sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and sales channel performance.

The project applies descriptive analysis to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights and recommendations.

📁 Data Understanding

* Dataset: Vrinda Store product sales data for 2022
* Rows: 31,048
* Columns: 15
* Analysis Tool: Microsoft Excel
* Analytical Technique: Descriptive Analysis

🎯 Objective

The objective of this analysis is to gain insights into the performance of different products and sales channels across various states and identify patterns that can support business decision-making.

❓ Business Questions

The analysis aimed to answer the following questions:

1. How many orders were successfully delivered?
2. Which gender purchased the highest quantity of products?
3. How many products were sold, and how much revenue did they generate?
4. Which product category generated the highest sales?
5. Which sales channel recorded the highest sales performance?
6. How many orders were delivered, returned, cancelled, and refunded?
7. Which product categories and sizes performed best?

🧹 Data Preparation

Microsoft Excel was used for:

* Data cleaning and preparation
* Data organization
* Descriptive analysis
* Pivot Tables
* Data visualization
* Dashboard creation

🔍 Analytical Technique

Descriptive Analysis

The analysis focused on summarizing historical sales data to identify patterns and trends in product sales, customer demographics, order status, sales channels, and revenue.

📈 Key Findings

Product Performance

Set was the highest revenue-generating product category, selling 12,446 units and generating approximately ₹10.6 million in revenue.

Kurta followed, selling 10,541 units and generating approximately ₹5.07 million in revenue.

Product Category Performance

The Set category recorded the highest share of sales at approximately 40%, followed by Kurta at 34%.

Other product categories also contributed to overall sales performance.

Sales Channel Performance

Amazon generated the highest revenue among the sales channels analyzed.

The Set category was also the highest-selling product category through Amazon.

Customer Gender

Female customers recorded the highest number of orders, with approximately 21,702 orders, compared with 9,535 orders from male customers.

This indicates that female customers represented the larger share of the customer base in the dataset.

Product Size

Medium (M) recorded the highest number of orders, with 5,526 orders, followed by Large (L) with 5,173 orders and Extra Large (XL) with 4,463 orders.

💼 Business Benefits

The analysis provides insights that can help the business to:

* Understand product and customer performance
* Identify high-performing products and sales channels
* Improve inventory and product planning
* Identify opportunities for business expansion
* Support data-driven decision-making
* Improve marketing and sales strategies

💡 Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following recommendations were made:

1. Expand into additional states where sales performance indicates strong market potential.
2. Increase staffing and operational capacity as the business expands.
3. Maximize seasonal and holiday sales through targeted promotional campaigns.
4. Increase marketing efforts toward male customers to improve the balance of customer purchases.
5. Strengthen online advertising and brand awareness across major sales channels.
6. Explore opportunities for international expansion after evaluating suitable markets.
7. Collaborate with reliable delivery partners to improve order fulfillment and customer satisfaction.
